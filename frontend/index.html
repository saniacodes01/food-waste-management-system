How it works

STEP 1

Restaurant posts food

A hotel, canteen or event lists leftover food with a pickup point and best-before time.

STEP 2

Nearby NGO claims it

NGOs see donations near them ranked by distance and claim what they can distribute.

STEP 3

Nearest partner delivers

The closest available delivery partner is auto-assigned to move it from the restaurant to the NGO.

STEP 4

It reaches the needy

The NGO confirms receipt. Every step is tracked end to end.
