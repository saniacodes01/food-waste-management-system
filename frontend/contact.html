Help & FAQs

Register as a restaurant, open your dashboard, choose "Post food", fill in the details and submit. Nearby NGOs will see it immediately.

Claimed donations are collected by a delivery partner and taken to the NGO, which distributes the food to needy people the same day.

Add a realistic "best before" time when posting. For safety, don't donate food that is already past its expiry date.
