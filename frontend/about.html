About Food Donate

A food waste management system that connects surplus food with the people who need it.

India produces a huge amount of food and also wastes a huge amount of it. Food Donate is a small attempt to close that gap: instead of leftover food from hotels, canteens, marriage halls and events going to waste, it is routed to NGOs and charities who distribute it to needy people the same day.

🍽️ Restaurants

Anyone with surplus food registers and posts a donation with a pickup location and a best-before time.

🤝 NGOs

Charities register directly, see donations near them ranked by distance, and claim what they can distribute.

🛵 Delivery partners

Volunteers and riders sign up; the nearest available partner is automatically matched to each pickup.

🛡️ Admin

Oversees accounts and donations, and can step in to reassign a delivery when needed.
